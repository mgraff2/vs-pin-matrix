1.3.2

  • Fixed: typing into another mod's map-screen panel could open Pin Matrix. Reported against Boat Autopilot — naming a route in its route planner opened the editor on the p. The map button's P shortcut now stands aside whenever any text box has keyboard focus, so it applies to chat, waypoint names, and any other mod that puts an input on the map screen. Pressing P with the map itself focused still opens the editor, and the rebindable Settings → Controls hotkey was never affected.
  • New setting MapButtonShortcutKey (default on) turns the P shortcut off entirely, for anyone who would rather keep the key free. The fix above works without it.
  • Now verified against dedicated servers for every release from 1.22.0 to 1.22.7, solo and alongside each supported companion mod.

1.3.1

  • Fixed the "Group duplicates" label wrapping onto a second line and overrunning the table header. Refresh moved down to the utilities row next to the recycle bin to make room.

1.3.0

  • Duplicate grouping and one-click cleanup. A Group duplicates switch folds pins that are identical in every column into a single row marked × N copies. Fix duplicates is labelled with the live count and removes every extra copy through the usual confirmation preview, always keeping the original. It scans your whole list, not just the filtered view, so a filter can't leave copies behind.
  • The colour filter shows the colours. Painted swatches with hex and a live count, sorted round the colour wheel, listing only colours your pins actually use. Counts respond to your other filters.
  • Fixed an empty table on servers where you hadn't opened the map yet — the mod now asks for your waypoints itself instead of waiting for the map to do it.
  • Fixed a crash on opening the editor caused by waypoint icons whose art hadn't loaded yet. An icon that genuinely can't be drawn now falls back to its name and stays usable.
